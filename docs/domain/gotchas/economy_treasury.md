# PILLAR II: ECONOMY & TREASURY INVARIANTS

> **Scope**: `src/domain/property_*`, `src/domain/treasury_*`, `src/domain/bond_*`, `src/domain/insolvency_*`

---

1. **Treasury Conservation Invariant**: Total game currency is conserved: $\Delta \text{System} = \Delta \text{Players} + \Delta \text{Treasury}$. All financial penalties (storm repairs, taxes, audit bailouts) not paid to another player MUST flow into `Treasury`. Currency destruction or phantom leaks are strictly forbidden.
2. **Insolvent Entity Role Guard**: An entity with negative balance (`balance < 0` in `InsolvencyPhase`) is STRICTLY FORBIDDEN from acting as `buyer` in P2P trades or trading assets with negative net cash (`price <= 0`). Insolvent entities may only act as `seller` with positive cash proceeds (`price > 0`) for solvency rescue.
3. **Dynamic Pricing SSOT**: Hardcoding property prices (e.g. literal 600) in client or UI logic is prohibited. Property values must be retrieved from the authoritative dictionary `PROPERTY_DEEDS.get(cellIndex)?.price`.
4. **Pre-Consumption Valuation Invariant**: When activating fee exemptions or card waivers (e.g. `CC_DIPLOMATIC` rent waiver), the system must compute base gross rent (`potentialRent = calculateRent(...)`) prior to card consumption to record actual saved amounts explicitly in narrative logs.
5. **Mortgaged Property P2P Debt Migration SSOT**: When mortgaged properties are traded via P2P (floor price 35% instead of 70%), principal loan liability (`mortgageLoans[cell]`) and mortgaged portfolio membership (`mortgagedProperties`) must migrate atomically from seller to buyer in `executeP2PTrade`, preserving total room debt invariants.
6. **Auction Clean Title & Senior Treasury Payoff SSOT [IMP-205]**: Upon property auction settlement (foreclosure or unowned sale), the winning bidder MUST receive an unencumbered clean title (`isMortgaged = false`, removing `unbuiltRounds`). For foreclosed debtor assets, Treasury is senior lienholder: winning bid proceeds pay off outstanding mortgage loan balances first (`loanPayoff = Math.min(winningBid, loan)` to `room.treasury`), returning surplus proceeds to debtor. Unowned land auctions remit 100% to `room.treasury`.
7. **Utility Revenue & Anti-Camping SSOT [IMP-214]**: The two utility tiles (Tile 12 EVN & Tile 28 Viettel) utilize flat landing fees (1 owned: 1,000, 2 owned: 2,500, upgraded Smart Grid/5G: 3,500 M VND), eliminating archaic 1935 2D6 dice formulas. Network toll collection (EVN electricity bills on opponent passing GO: C1=100, C2=200, C3=300 M/tile, C0=0 M; Viettel telecom fee of 150 M on drawing Chance/Market cards) MUST adhere to 4 guards: (1) Locked while owner is in Audit (`inAudit || auditTurnsLeft > 0`); (2) Locked while utility is mortgaged (`owner.mortgagedProperties?.includes(...) || isMortgaged`); (3) Self-Billing Exemption; (4) Non-inflationary Sink during debtor insolvency: debtor is debited to negative balance, utility owner collects only debtor's available liquid cash, preventing phantom money creation.
8. **Auction Invariant Conservation & Off-Turn Actor Parity [IMP-216]**:
   - **Closed System Conservation**: Real estate auctions transfer cash from the winning bidder into Treasury or clear debt obligations ($\Delta \text{System} = 0$). Telemetry Watchdog `computeCellDelta` must not subtract winning bids from expected system money when the room is active (`delta.roomStarted === true`).
   - **Watchdog Bot Actor Parity**: In auctions following a decline (`declinedPlayerId`), the declining player is barred from bidding. Bots placing bids act as out-of-turn actors (`highestBidderId`). Telemetry Watchdog must inspect `delta.auction?.highestBidderId` independently to prevent spurious bot burst loop alerts.
9. **Corporate Bond Tranches, Collateral Selection & Interest Payoff SSOT [IMP-217]**:
   - **Optimal Collateral Allocation**: When issuing corporate bonds across 3 Tranches (`WORKING_CAPITAL` 20% NW, `EXPANSION` 40% NW, `ALL_IN` 60% NW), collateral selection sorts ascending by property price (`sort((a,b) => priceA - priceB)`), locking only the minimum cheapest properties needed to meet collateral ratios and $\ge 2$ properties (except `ALL_IN` which locks all unencumbered properties). Surplus properties retain 100% freedom to mortgage, unmortgage, or trade via P2P.
   - **Treasury Conservation on Repayment**: On early repayment (`handleRepayBond`) and final maturity (`processBondTurnTransition`), Treasury ONLY collects the actual interest differential (`interestPaid = Math.max(0, repayAmount - principal)`), eliminating flat 20% hardcoding that caused phantom Treasury inflation on lower-rate tranches (8%, 15%).
   - **Backward Compatibility Fallback**: When `trancheId === undefined` (legacy FSM or legacy bot callers), the system applies default 80% NW, 3-round maturity, 20% interest, locking all clean real estate, preserving `TC-192C` test contracts.
10. **Land Registration Fee SSOT & Visual Parity Invariant**: Tile 04 (`CellType.Tax`) applies progressive taxation by ability to pay: 10% of player liquid cash (capped at 2,000 M VND, remitted to Treasury). 3D board textures (`tile_texture_data.ts`), action dock labels (`actionLabel`), and design specs must synchronously state `10% LIQUID CASH`, eliminating desync with obsolete static labels `PAY 1,000 M`.
11. **Financial Activity Log Collision & Pure Multi-Transaction Disambiguation [IMP-225]**:
    - **Position Store Parity vs. Pure Delta**: To identify passing GO reliably without triggering phantom salaries for non-moving players (landlords receiving rent or M&A victims), compare `prevPos = prevState.playerPositions?.[p.id]` with `newPos = nextState.playerPositions?.[p.id] ?? p.position`. When `prevPos === newPos`, the player did not traverse the board.
    - **Net Zero Disambiguation**: A player passing GO (+2,000) and landing on rent equal to 2,000 produces net diff = 0. Activity hook `detectFinancialAndStatusActivities` must check `(prevP.balance !== p.balance || hasPassedGo)` and emit synthetic `diff: 0` entries so both `salary` (+2,000) and `rent` (-2,000) generate corresponding logs.
    - **Multi-Deduction vs. Insolvency Disambiguation**: When enduring multiple deductions in 1 tick (rent + overdraft repayment), `ownerReceiver.diff <= rentAmount` does NOT imply insolvency if `payerBalance >= 0`. Only tag `(insolvent)` when `payerBalance < 0` actually holds, preserving remaining cash for subsequent fee handlers.
    - **Pure Functional Immutability**: Preserve `readonly diff` in `BalanceDelta`, avoiding in-place mutations and propagating remaining balances via pure pipelines.
12. **Escalating Audit Bailout & Transparent Dock Affordance SSOT [IMP-226]**:
    - **Escalating Penalty Schedule**: Bailout fees and mandatory release penalties after 3 failed doubles scale by prior violation counts (`auditCount`): 1st: 500 M, 2nd: 1,000 M, 3rd+: 2,000 M VND (`calculateBailAmount(auditCount)` SSOT in `src/domain/property_rent.ts`). Replaces complex, unrealistic `10% Net Worth` formulas.
    - **Counter Lifecycle Singularity**: `auditCount` increments solely upon entering audit in `sendToAudit(room, playerId)`. Handlers `handleBailOut` and `handleAuditTurnTransition` must never increment `auditCount`.
    - **Bot AI & UI Transparent Affordance**: Bot AI (`decideAuditBailout`) uses `calculateBailAmount` to evaluate affordability and safety margins (`minBuffer`). `ActionDock` displays dynamic bailout tiers transparently (`Bailout (500)`, `Bailout - 2nd (1,000)`, `Bailout - Repeat (2,000)`), disabling buttons on insufficient funds with descriptive tooltips.
13. **Zero-Delta Event Suppression & Audit Teleportation Immunity [IMP-228]**:
    - **Deceptive Trap**: Assuming that when a player has unchanged balance (`diff === 0`), checking `hasPassedGo` alone reliably captures GO salary collisions (`TC-225.03`), and fallback `processPayerFee` handles remaining fees.
    - **Physical Finding (Tick #316)**: When a pawn in the second half of the board (e.g. Tile 28) is sent to Audit Tile 10 (`inAudit: true`), condition `10 <= 28` caused `checkPassedGo(28, 10)` to return `true`. Unchanged balance (`diff = 0`) queued Bot into `payers` with `{ diff: 0 }`. In `extractPassedGoActivities`, guard `isSentToAudit` suppressed salary, letting phantom debt `{ diff: 0 }` leak to `processPayerFee`. Lacking an `absDiff <= 0` guard, it rendered nonsensical `"Bot paid fee / tax 0"` and emitted a 0-VND Floating Badge penalty.
    - **Verified Invariants**:
      (1) *Audit Exclusion*: In `collectPayersAndReceivers`, `hasPassedGo` must explicitly exclude being sent to audit: `const hasPassedGo = !isSentToAudit && prevPos !== undefined && newPos !== undefined && prevPos !== newPos && checkPassedGo(prevPos, newPos);`.
      (2) *Zero-Delta Suppression*: All financial change formatting handlers (`processPayerFee`, `processReceiverReward`) MUST include an early guard: `if (Math.abs(amount) <= 0) return null;`. Zero-value changes must never emit logs or badges.
14. **Utility Monopoly Upgrade Requirement & Rent De-escalation Fallback [IMP-247]**:
    - **Deceptive Trap (ROI Paradox & Arbitrage Cashout)**: Allowing standalone utility upgrades or retaining 3,500 M rent when a utility in the group is mortgaged enables arbitrage: upgrade EVN (1,000 M), mortgage Viettel to extract 500 M cash, yet continue collecting 3,500 M risk-free.
    - **Verified Invariant**: Upgrading to Smart Grid/5G requires owning both Utilities with neither mortgaged. If either is mortgaged or transferred, landing fees automatically de-escalate to 1,000 M VND. `[DOMAIN/ECONOMIC]`
15. **Macroeconomic Policy Polarity & Opposing Modifier Resolution SSOT [IMP-275]**:
    - **Opposing Policy Paradox**: When two opposing macroeconomic cards coexist (Credit Stimulus `MC_CREDIT_STIMULUS` loose vs. Rate Hike `MC_RATE_HIKE` tight):
      (1) *Mortgage Interest Tier (`getMortgageInterestRate`)*: Stimulus policies take absolute precedence (`if (stimulusActive) return 0`). Pass-GO interest rate remains 0%, eliminating the 10% rate penalty from `MC_RATE_HIKE`.
      (2) *Building Upgrade Cost Tier (`calculateUpgradeCost`)*: Multipliers compound sequentially: base cost multiplied by 0.8, then by 1.2 $\rightarrow$ Effective cost: `Math.floor(Math.floor(base * 0.8) * 1.2)` (approximately 0.96x, a 4% net reduction).
      (3) *UI Affordance Parity*: All UI cost and condition evaluators (`title_deed_affordance.ts`) MUST invoke authoritative `calculateUpgradeCost(cellIndex, currentLevel, activeModifiers)` rather than reading static array `deed.upgradeCosts`, eliminating phantom button rejections when server costs shift under macro policies. `[DOMAIN/ECONOMIC]`
16. **Pass-GO Multi-Transaction Decomposition & Zero-Falsy Attribution SSOT [IMP-289]**:
    - **Deceptive Trap**: When passing GO triggers multiple financial obligations simultaneously (Salary + Overdraft debt `CC_OVERDRAFT` + Credit interest `CC_FREE_CREDIT` + Building property tax + Mortgage interest), absolute numeric matching `diff === X` in fallback `processPayerFee` collapses due to cross-netting. Surplus differentials get misattributed to "Land Tax ➔ Treasury" and trigger Tile 04 formulas errantly.
    - **Verified Invariants**:
      (1) *Sequential Accounting Decomposition*: All financial obligations arising from crossing GO MUST be decomposed sequentially in `extractPassedGoActivities` (extracting Salary, Overdraft, Credit Interest, Property Taxes, and Mortgage Interest sequentially from payer `diff`). Netting or relying on `absDiff` in `processPayerFee` is prohibited.
      (2) *Zero-Falsy Numeric Guard*: Numeric fields that may legitimately equal 0 (e.g. `overdraftRoundsLeft`) MUST check `!== undefined`, never relying on implicit falsy checks `?(prop)`.
      (3) *Causal Spatio-Temporal Binding*: Deductions from crossing GO must anchor visually to `getPawnPassGoDelay` with staggered offsets (+200ms), never drifting into `getPawnLandingDelay`. `[DOMAIN/ECONOMIC]`
17. **Characterization Baseline Invariant & 40-Cell Snapshot [IMP-OBSERVABILITY]**:
    - **Deceptive Trap (Silent Economic Drift)**: Rent, auction, mortgage, utility, and property tax formulas span 40 tiles across improvement tiers (Tier 0 to Tier 4 / Resort). Refactoring or introducing new economic modifiers while testing only 1–2 sample tiles (e.g. Tile 1 or Tile 39) risks silent formula drift on remaining tiles, destabilizing game balance.
    - **Verified Invariants**:
      (1) *40-Cell Matrix Snapshot*: Any modification to fee schedules, rent calculations (`calculateRent`), auctions (`auction`), mortgages (`mortgage`), or land taxes MUST execute or establish a 40-cell characterization snapshot test prior to edits as an authoritative baseline.
      (2) *100% Baseline Behavior Preservation*: Refactored code must match 100% of baseline values under neutral conditions (0 modifiers). Deliberate balance adjustments must be declared explicitly in specifications with Product Owner approval. `[DOMAIN/ECONOMIC]`
