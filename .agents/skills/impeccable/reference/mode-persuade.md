# Persuade and Experience

On Persuade and Experience surfaces the visitor judges the impression and feeling, so the visual world may own and structure the presentation.

## Directions

The surface may carry its native grammar across navigation, content, controls, and states, within platform usability conventions.

For **Persuade**, the opening must make the offer intelligible and desirable, expose a clear action, and demonstrate something unique. The action is the one visitors came to take: play game, join room, purchase. Conversion lives inside the form's own vocabulary: a hook that lands clearly, a visible primary action, a legible reading order.

For **Experience** (splash screens, victory/game-over screens, celebratory modals), the work itself leads from the first viewport.

## Comps & Layouts

The visual world may be the carrier of the page, never merely a flat picture of it. Material coverage is intentional: the world's material is the ground and major surfaces, its typography sets the headlines.

The world carries the page's parts and never absorbs them:
- An action on a button or plaque must remain plainly clickable.
- Text must read with high contrast against whatever sits behind it.
- Sections keep a steady vertical rhythm.
- The primary call-to-action is unmistakable.
- Avoid both extremes: an un-interactive painted poster, and a generic template with random stickers.
